FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as an organ donor?

Go to the dashboard and click "Register Donor" under the Donors menu. Fill in your medical details, blood group, and select which organs you wish to pledge.

How does the compatibility test work?

Our system performs cross-match and HLA typing analysis between donors and recipients. A score above 80% is considered highly compatible.

Can my hospital join the OrganLife network?

Yes! Email us at hospitals@organlife.in or use the contact form with subject "Hospital Partnership". We'll onboard your team within 5 working days.

What is a donation chain?

A donation chain links multiple donor-recipient pairs in sequence — allowing living donors to trigger a cascade of transplants across different hospitals.

Is my data secure?

All data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL database with proper access controls. Patient and donor records are only accessible to authorised medical staff.

How do I request an organ for a patient?

Use the "Request Organ" form in the Recipients section of the dashboard. Include the patient's blood group, organ needed, urgency level, and hospital details.
