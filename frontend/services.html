Core Services

Everything You Need

A complete suite of tools built around your PostgreSQL database — managing the full organ donation lifecycle.

🫀

Donor Registration

Register living and deceased donors with full medical profiles — blood group, HLA type, organ preferences, and next-of-kin details. Data flows directly into the donor, donor_living, and donor_deceased tables.

donor table donor_living donor_deceased
🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Recipient Management

Add and manage organ recipients with urgency levels, blood groups, and regional assignments. Supports north, south, east, and west region databases with priority-based waitlist management.

recipient recipient_north/south/east/west waitlist
🧬

Compatibility Testing

Run cross-match and HLA typing tests between donors and recipients. Our system scores compatibility and flags borderline or incompatible pairs, reducing surgical risk and improving transplant success rates.

compatibility_test
🔗

Donation Chain Coordination

Build and manage multi-donor organ chains — linking living donors, hospitals, and recipients in sequence. Each link is tracked in real time ensuring no organ is wasted and chains complete successfully.

donation_chain chain_link
🫁

Organ Inventory Tracking

Real-time tracking of harvested organs — type, viability window, transport status, and hospital location. Critical for time-sensitive organs like hearts (4hr window) and livers (12hr window).

organ table
💉

Transplant Records

Full end-to-end transplant documentation — linking donor, recipient, organ, surgeon, and hospital. Post-operative details including immunosuppressant regimens, blood loss, duration, and follow-up dates are tracked separately.

transplant_record transplant_medical transplant_details
🏨

Hospital Network

Manage your partner hospital network — capacity, active operations, state coverage, and geographic coordinates. Connect hospitals to donors, recipients, and surgical staff for seamless coordination.

hospital table
👨‍⚕️

Medical Staff Directory

Maintain a complete directory of surgeons, cardiologists, nephrologists, and support staff. Track specialisations, on-call availability, transplant counts, and hospital assignments.

medical_staff table
